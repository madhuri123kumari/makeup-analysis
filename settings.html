LEGAL INFORMATION

Privacy Policy

AI Makeup Analysis Guide is an educational application designed to provide makeup analysis, recommendations and related features.

Information Used by the Application

Information entered by the user, such as profile details and preferences, may be used to provide the application's features.

User Control

Users can review and update available profile information from the Profile section of the application.

Uploaded Images

Images should only be uploaded when the user has the necessary permission to use them. Images should be handled according to the application's intended analysis workflow.

This page describes the privacy approach for this educational capstone project and is not a substitute for a formal legal policy.

APPLICATION TERMS

Terms & Conditions

By using AI Makeup Analysis Guide, users agree to use the application responsibly and for its intended educational purpose.

Acceptable Use

  • Use the application lawfully and responsibly.
  • Only upload images and information that you are permitted to use.
  • Do not attempt to misuse or disrupt application features.
  • Review AI-generated recommendations before acting on them.

Educational Purpose

This application is developed as an educational capstone project. AI-generated beauty suggestions are informational and should not be treated as professional medical or dermatological advice.
